## 4. French language subject

The 2026–2027 first-term Ministry Grade 5 catalogue lists English as the first foreign language and does not list a standalone French student textbook among its 15 listed books. Separate Grade 5 Ministry weekly assessments are published for French first and second foreign language. This is evidence of assessment tracks, not evidence of the book Girard assigns.

The four current Miss Noor French-language lessons therefore remain **UNVERIFIED** until the exact Girard book/series and first-/second-foreign-language track are established.
