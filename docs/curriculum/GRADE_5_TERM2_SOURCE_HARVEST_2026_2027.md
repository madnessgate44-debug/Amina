## 5. Source retrieval protocol for each subject

OpenHands must independently check and either confirm or correct every row above. For each subject, retrieve and preserve metadata for:

1. Official student book for Grade 5, 2026–2027, Term 2 (exact title, language, edition/imprint, URL, PDF page count, SHA-256).
2. Official term distribution / scope and sequence.
3. Official performance-assessment or weekly classroom/home assessment PDFs.
4. Official teacher guide, if published.
5. Girard's assigned booklist, term plan, timetable, or written school confirmation.
6. Exact unit → lesson → printed page range → stated objectives → assessment mapping.

A source counts as **retrieved and inspected** only after the actual PDF/file is opened or downloaded and its title page/imprint, contents, relevant lesson pages and page count are inspected. A search result, filename, catalogue card or URL alone is a lead, not a fetched curriculum.

For each item, record source authority, publication year, term, grade, language, track, edition, retrieval date, page count, SHA-256, inspected page ranges, and confidence. Keep Ministry baseline, Girard assignment, and Miss Noor-authored teaching material in separate columns.
