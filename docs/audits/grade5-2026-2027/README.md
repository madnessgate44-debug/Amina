# Grade 5 Term 1 curriculum source audit (2026–2027)

This directory contains the evidence-first source and registry reconciliation for Egyptian Primary Grade 5, academic year 2026–2027, Term 1.

## Files

- `subject-source-audit.json` — source URLs, retrieval timestamps, HTTP results, byte counts, SHA-256, PDF metadata, page counts, extraction methods and OCR counts for all attempted sources.
- `subject-page-fingerprints.csv` — one row per successful PDF page with normalized extracted/OCR text SHA-256 and character count.
- `subject-page-headings.csv` — concise page-heading candidates derived from extracted/OCR text.
- `math-fr-lesson-reconciliation.csv` — all 46 French Mathematics StudentBook TOC lessons, current app-record matches and performance-assessment page candidates.
- `math-fr-extra-record-review.csv` — review of four legacy French-extract Math records.
- `math-fr-assessment-section-index.csv` — weekly/monthly/term assessment-section candidates from the French Math performance-assessment book.
- `english-unit-reconciliation.csv` — six English units and their 26 lesson/creative-task count.
- `english-lesson-reconciliation.csv` — all 26 Connect 5 StudentBook lessons/creative tasks, app-record matches and assessment-book page candidates.
- `english-lesson-content-review.csv` — concise, app-authored summaries of the 26 source lesson segments and skills.
- `french-language-assessment-track-matrix.csv` — source retrieval evidence for French1/French2 weekly assessments and unsuccessful standalone-book candidates.
- `french-language-weekly-content-review.csv` — OCR-based content summary for the 13 retrieved weekly assessments.
- `subject-status.md` — consolidated Science, French Mathematics, French language and English status, evidence limits, PR state and remaining gates.
- `continuation-2026-11-16.md` — dated continuation checkpoint and work log.
- `additional-subject-registry-inventory.md` — inventory of the remaining Grade 5 catalog subjects, current app registry modules, and unverified school-assignment/source gates.

## Source retrieval and extraction method

Source retrieval and page fingerprints were generated by GitHub Actions run [43844113846](https://github.com/madnessgate44-debug/Amina/actions/runs/43844113846).

- 21 Ministry-hosted URLs attempted; 17 PDFs retrieved.
- 448 pages fingerprinted.
- OCR attempted on 165 pages; 163 pages produced OCR text.
- Poppler `pdfinfo` / `pdftotext -layout` supplied PDF metadata and text extraction.
- Tesseract OCR language combinations: French Mathematics `fra+eng`; English `eng+ara`; French-language weekly assessments `fra+ara`.
- All 46 retrieved French1/French2 weekly-assessment pages were OCR-processed. Low-text pages in the Math and English StudentBooks and assessment books received OCR fallback.
- SHA-256 hashes are calculated over normalized extracted/OCR page text.

A one-day Actions artifact from run 43844113846 contains the temporary `instructional-page-text-review.txt` and `toc-review.txt` used for lesson review. It expires on 17 November 2026 at approximately 10:00 UTC. Full page text is not committed to this repository. The artifact is a temporary review aid, not a durable textbook copy.

## Interpretation limits

- Ministry-hosted URL retrieval does not independently authenticate publisher authorship. The retrieved PDFs show third-party PDF-processing metadata.
- Page fingerprints compare normalized extracted/OCR text only; they do not prove byte identity, visual/layout identity, diagram fidelity or pedagogical equivalence.
- OCR text can contain recognition/layout errors. Page-heading and lesson summaries are source-navigation aids, not a substitute for visual review of diagrams, tables and answer choices.
- Weekly French assessments establish two available tracks (French1 and French2), not which track École Girard assigns. They are not a complete French StudentBook.
- TOC, lesson-title, objective and assessment-page mapping is not proof that every worked example, exercise, answer key or teaching interaction has been reviewed.
- Source coverage does not establish classroom pacing or learner mastery.
- App-authored teaching material must be labelled as such; do not present it as verbatim textbook content or an authenticated Ministry teacher guide.

## Copyright and privacy

Do not add source PDFs, full extracted textbook text, full OCR text, private learner information, credentials or secrets. Store concise paraphrased summaries, page references, metadata, hashes, reconciliation matrices and original app-authored teaching material only.
